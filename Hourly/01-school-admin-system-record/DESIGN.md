# GoodHours Formal Ad Design

## Style Prompt
A formal, school-admin-first ad for GoodHours. The look should feel like an official school brochure: bright, clean, trustworthy, and composed — not salesy, not playful.

## Colors
- Paper white: #F8FAFC
- Administrative blue: #1D4ED8
- Deep navy: #0F172A
- Gold accent: #D4AF37
- Soft sky tint: #E8F1FF

## Typography
- Headlines: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif
- Body: Inter, system-ui, Arial, sans-serif
- Numbers / labels: bold, high-contrast, tabular where possible

## Motion
- Gentle fades and measured slides
- Calm, authoritative pacing
- No flashy neon transitions
- No bouncy or playful motion

## What NOT to Do
- No dark gamer aesthetics
- No generic startup landing-page blue
- No slang or casual copy
- No cluttered layouts
- No loud or chaotic motion
