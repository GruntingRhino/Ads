GoodHours Social Ad Plan

Goal
- Create short vertical social content that shows GoodHours as the simple way to manage student volunteer hours.

Target audience
- School admins
- Community service coordinators
- Students who need a clean way to log or find hours

Core message
- Students need hours fast.
- Schools need cleaner tracking.
- GoodHours removes spreadsheet chaos.

Ad structure
1. Hook (0-5s)
   - "Students need hours. Fast."
   - Show urgency and pain point.

2. Problem/Solution (5-10s)
   - "Schools get cleaner tracking."
   - Emphasize less admin friction.

3. Trust/Workflow (10-15s)
   - "Students, parents, and staff stay in sync."
   - Show that the workflow is simple for everyone.

4. CTA (15-20s)
   - "Built for real schools."
   - "Book a demo • Start faster with GoodHours"

Creative direction
- Vertical 9:16 format
- Bold white text on dark branded backgrounds
- Strong contrast, minimal copy, punchy transitions
- Clean, school-friendly, modern feel
