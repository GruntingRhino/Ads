# GoodHours Clean Live-Site Ad Design

## Style Prompt
A clean, formal, school-admin-first product ad built from the live GoodHours homepage. The look should feel authentic to the real site: white space, blue-first trust branding, navy type, subtle gold accents, and dashboard proof. It should feel like a polished school software homepage, not a generic startup ad.

## Colors
- Paper white: #F8FAFC
- GoodHours blue: #1D4ED8
- Deep navy: #0F172A
- Gold accent: #D4AF37
- Soft sky tint: #E8F1FF
- Muted gray: #94A3B8

## Typography
- Headlines: Inter, system-ui, Arial, sans-serif; bold and compact
- Body: Inter, system-ui, Arial, sans-serif; clean and readable
- UI labels / numbers: bold, tabular where possible

## Motion
- Calm, confident, product-tour pacing
- Smooth fades and measured slide-ins
- Subtle zooms on live screenshots only
- No bouncy motion, no flashy transitions, no gimmicks

## What NOT to Do
- No cramped card grids
- No serif brochure look
- No generic SaaS blue gradients everywhere
- No cluttered overlays on top of screenshots
- No fake-looking mockups that don’t resemble the live site
- No aggressive neon, glassmorphism, or playful motion
