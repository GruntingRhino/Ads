# LiveImproved Ad Research Notes

## What high-performing ads usually do
- Hook immediately in the first 1–2 seconds.
- Lead with a pain point, identity claim, or contrarian truth.
- Keep one clear promise per ad.
- Show proof fast: app UI, numbers, before/after, or a concrete consequence.
- End with a sharp CTA.

## Layout patterns that work
- One dominant focal point per scene.
- One support cluster per scene.
- Big headline, short subhead, tiny proof labels.
- Dark background + bright accent for contrast.
- Framed screenshots / device mockups for credibility.
- Negative space around the core message.

## Voice and language
- Direct second-person language: `you`, not generic audience talk.
- Short, punchy clauses.
- 3–8 words per beat is ideal.
- Use concrete nouns and numbers.
- Avoid vague motivation and fluffy startup language.
- Sound confident, not needy.

## Hook formulas
- `You are not X. You are Y.`
- `Stop doing X. Start doing Y.`
- `POV: you finally got tired of X.`
- `The problem is not X. It is Y.`
- `If you do not track it, you do not control it.`

## Motion / transitions
- Fast fades and push-ins.
- Scene crossfades, not hard cuts.
- Small zooms and drift keep static frames alive.
- Animate cards, chips, bars, and CTA highlights.
- Use a sheen or pulse so the CTA feels satisfying.

## Do not do
- Do not overload a scene with too many ideas.
- Do not write paragraphs.
- Do not use soft pastel productivity visuals.
- Do not make the CTA timid.
- Do not let the ad feel like a feature dump.
