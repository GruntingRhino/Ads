# LiveImproved Design System

## Style Prompt
A masculine, aggressive, high-performance self-improvement brand. The visuals should feel like a private operating system for men who want to stop drifting and start measuring life.

## Colors
- Background: #070B10
- Surface: #111823
- Steel: #1B2633
- Electric blue accent: #5F7CFF
- Alert red: #FF4D4D
- Success mint: #8EF2A8
- Soft text: #EAF0F7

## Typography
- Headlines: Impact / Arial Black / condensed uppercase styling
- Body: Inter / system sans-serif
- Numbers: tabular, bold, high contrast

## Motion
- Fast, decisive entrances
- Short pauses on proof points
- Sharp crossfades between scenes
- No floaty, cheerful easing

## What NOT to Do
- No soft pastel productivity vibes
- No generic startup blue-on-white layouts
- No playful bubbly UI
- No weak CTA language like “try it out” or “maybe download”
- No cluttered paragraphs
